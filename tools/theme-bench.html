Theme design bench

Browser-side preview of the P4 UI, built from the firmware's own numbers: coordinates from ui_tune.h, palette hex from theme_t, and the real Jost Bold face. Every control maps to one knob that also exists on the device under Settings › DEVELOPER, and the panel at the bottom emits ui_tune.h lines in the exact format the device's EXPORT TO SERIAL prints — so the bench, the panel and the repo all speak the same values.

Workflow: rough the look out here (instant, no flash), paste the emitted block into ui_tune.h, build once, then fine-tune on the real panel via the DEVELOPER tab and EXPORT the result back into the repo. v2 is the current committed BOLD; v1 is the first pass, kept for comparison; BASIC is the baseline theme. Cover art here is abstract placeholder, not real album art.
